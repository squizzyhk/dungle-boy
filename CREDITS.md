Original files and License.txt files are preserved under assets/sfx-audition/<pack>/. The audition manifest records SHA-256 hashes. Previews were decoded to mono 44.1 kHz PCM16 and peak-normalized; jump variants combine the two credited sources. Footstep audition previews repeat four steps, while the selected source is the single footstep_concrete_000.ogg.

Landing: **Hitting Bed with Pillow.wav** by **krnash**, https://freesound.org/people/krnash/sounds/389799/, licensed CC0-1.0. Uses the publicly available HQ MP3 preview, excerpt 1.65–2.25 seconds, slowed to 80% speed, low-pass filtered at 1600 Hz, faded and peak-normalized to -9 dBFS. Source URL, download URL, hash and edit recipe are preserved in assets/sfx-audition/landing-round2/sources.json. No audition padding is present in the game asset.
