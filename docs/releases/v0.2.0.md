AYYY. **little marshmallow, big space trip.**

dungle boy has a new orbital look, a softer little stride, and sounds for the whole run. the three courses have been rebuilt around the new world, and we've got a fresh box of obstacles and pickups ready for the next batch of levels.

the big stuff:

- **out among the planets.** lavender skies, floating towers, glowing platforms and a proper finish beacon. orbital garden, relay heights and stardust sprint all got rebuilt routes and collectible trails
- **tiny legs putting in work.** our marshmallow has a new running animation, a little bounce in the belly, and squash and stretch when you jump and land. same little dude on the menu and in the game
- **the run has a sound now.** jumps, landings, footsteps, coins, boosts and the finish all have their own cues. buttons get feedback too, and the sound you hear when a run ends follows what caught you
- **more toys for the next levels.** reactor crates, crystal clusters, retracting spikes, a plasma rotor, a boost pad and three power-up orbs. eight reusable pieces, with animated sheets ready to build from
- **back to the menu.** the menu button after a failed run works again, with a little more room around it

FYI the new asset kit is stocked, but those new traps, speed pads, shields and magnets haven't been added to playable courses yet. their gameplay rules come with the next level work. this drop rebuilds the three existing courses, it doesn't add another set. sound levels may still get a little tuning as we play.

full nerd detail in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/squizzyhk/dungle-boy/blob/v0.2.0/CHANGELOG.md).
