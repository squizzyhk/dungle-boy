yo yo. **new worlds, sharper obstacles, our own theme.**

the little dude has two proper new places to run. level one stays familiar, then you're out across crystal aqueducts and into an ember foundry with more things trying to catch your feet. and the menus have a tune now.

the big stuff:

- **two fresh courses.** crystal aqueduct brings mint skies, waterfalls and separated islands. ember foundry turns up the heat with a new industrial backdrop, tighter encounters and more hazards. both replace the old second and third courses
- **the toys are in the game.** reactor crates, crystal clusters, retracting spikes and spinning plasma rotors are now part of the runs. level two introduces six hazards; level three puts eleven in your way, including a rotor you can run underneath
- **hit the spike, not the air beside it.** contact follows the solid artwork and the current animation frame. transparent corners, soft glow and fully retracted spikes won't take you out
- **pickups do their thing.** speed pads give you a short burst, jump orbs lift your jumps, shields absorb one hit and magnets pull nearby coins towards you
- **dungle boy has a theme.** the new track loops in the main menu and the end-of-run screens. there's a music toggle, and the track pauses when you start playing

FYI level music is still coming later, so the courses keep their sound effects without a backing track for now. your browser may need a click or tap before menu music starts. orbital garden keeps its layout and pace, with the same more precise spike contact used across the game.
