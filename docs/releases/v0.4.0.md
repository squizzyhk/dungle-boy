# dungle boy 0.4.0: four themes in orbit

yo friends. **the menu playlist just got a whole lot bigger.**

dungle boy now rolls through all four themes in order, giving every menu visit more music while keeping the same simple on and off control.

the big stuff:

- **four tracks, one trip.** the original theme plays into theme 2, then 3, then 4, before returning to the original
- **the music keeps its place.** starting a run pauses the current track, and the next menu picks it back up from the same spot
- **one clean control.** the existing music button still controls the whole playlist without stacking extra copies
- **ready on the live site.** the complete playlist ships under `doblins.xyz/dungleboy/`

FYI this update adds menu music only. levels still use their sound effects without backing music, and browsers may need a click or tap before the playlist starts.

full nerd detail in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/squizzyhk/dungle-boy/blob/v0.4.0/CHANGELOG.md).
