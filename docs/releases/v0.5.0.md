# dungle boy 0.5.0: a closet for the flump

hey friends. **dungle boy's wardrobe finally has somewhere to hang.**

this update builds the system that will hold every hat, shirt and pair of shoes the boy earns. there's nothing to try on yet, but unlocks now have a home that survives between visits, and the game already knows its hats from its full outfits.

the big stuff:

- **four slots, ready and waiting.** hats, shirts, shoes and full outfits are proper categories now, with an empty catalog waiting for its first batch of gear
- **unlocks that stick around.** earned cosmetics save to the browser, so coming back later picks up right where you left off. this is dungle boy's first save file
- **full outfits know their job.** putting one on takes over the hat, shirt and shoes at once, and taking it off brings the individual pieces straight back
- **safe under pressure.** broken or missing save data falls back to a fresh start instead of making a mess, and the closet keeps working even when the browser refuses to save

FYI there are no cosmetic items in the game yet, so there's nothing to unlock, nothing to wear and no wardrobe screen to visit. the first batch of gear, the ways to earn it and that screen all come with future updates. nothing in this one changes how the boy runs or jumps.

full nerd detail in [CHANGELOG.md](https://github.com/squizzyhk/dungle-boy/blob/v0.5.0/CHANGELOG.md).
